## Game Overview

### Objective
Be the first player to reach **Tile 100**. No exact roll required - any roll that reaches or exceeds 100 wins!

### Turn Structure
1. Roll the dice (1-6)
2. Apply any status effects or bonuses
3. Move your piece
4. Resolve any encounters (dragons, monsters, portals, knights)
5. Next player's turn

## Dragons

Dragons act as "snakes" - landing on a dragon's head triggers a **defense check**. Fail, and you slide down to the tail!

| Dragon | Element | Head Tile | Tail Tile | Difficulty | Success Roll |
|--------|---------|-----------|-----------|------------|--------------|
| **Slime-Tooth** | Poison (Green) | 32 | 6 | Easy | 3-6 (67%) |
| **Frost-Fang** | Ice (Blue) | 62 | 19 | Medium | 4-6 (50%) |
| **Ignis the King** | Fire (Red) | 98 | 75 | Hard | 5-6 (33%) |

### Dragon Defense Mechanics
- Roll a D6 when landing on or passing through a dragon's head
- Meet or exceed the success threshold to stay
- Fail and you slide to the tail tile
- **Armor Shards** provide automatic success (consumed on use)
- **Reginald** gets +1 to all dragon defense rolls

| **Ice** | Frost-Fang | Slip (random 1 space forward or back) |

## Game Mechanics

### Movement
1. Roll D6
2. Apply status effects (burned -1, slowed x0.75, reversed = negative)
3. Apply bonuses (Speed Potion +3, Arcane Insight +1, etc.)
4. Move that many spaces
5. If passing through a dragon's head zone, make a defense check
6. Resolve the tile you land on

### Winning
- First player to reach or pass **Tile 100** wins!
- No exact roll required

### Multiplayer
- 2-6 players supported
- Online multiplayer via Socket.io
- Turn order is determined by player join order

### Tips
- **Armor Shards** are crucial for late-game dragons (especially Ignis!)
- **Portals** can be game-changers - know your risk tolerance
- **Knights** are always safe - aim for them!
- **Character abilities** should influence your strategy
- **Items** can save you from disaster - don't forget to use them!
